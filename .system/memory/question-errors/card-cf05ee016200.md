## Prompt
Defining total asset turnover as revenue divided by average total assets, all else equal, impairment write-downs of long-lived assets owned by a company will most likely result in an increase for that company in which ratio or ratios?

## Wrong Output
Selected B. The total asset turnover but not the debt-to-equity ratio.

## Choices
A. The debt-to-equity ratio but not the total asset turnover.
B. The total asset turnover but not the debt-to-equity ratio.
C. Both the debt-to-equity ratio and the total asset turnover.
