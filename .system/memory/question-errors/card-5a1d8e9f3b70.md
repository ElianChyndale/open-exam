## Prompt

The most likely reason for a country to encourage foreign direct investment while heavily restricting foreign investment in liquid domestic assets is to:

A. prevent capital flight.
B. generate an increase in the domestic interest rate.
C. stabilize the production capacity of the domestic country.

## Wrong Output

C. stabilize the production capacity — confused the effect of FDI (which does increase capacity) with the reason for the restriction structure.

## Root Cause

**concept_confusion**: 没区分两件事：
1. 限制 liquid asset investment → 防热钱突然流出（capital flight）
2. 鼓励 FDI → 增加生产产能（但**这是 FDI 的效果**，不是"限制 liquid + 鼓励 FDI"这个组合的理由）

题干问的是"为什么用这种组合政策"，核心目的是防 capital flight。FDI 本身确实 improve capacity，但那不是这个政策组合的**主要原因**。

## Correct Resolution

Restricting foreign investment in liquid assets (portfolio flows, hot money) prevents sudden reversal/capital flight. Simultaneously encouraging FDI (illiquid, long-term) keeps real investment coming. The pair targets **financial stability**, not production capacity per se.
