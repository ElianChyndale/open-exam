## Prompt
Purple Fleur S.A., a retailer of floral products, reported cost of goods sold for the year of USD75 million. Total assets increased by USD55 million, but inventory declined by USD6 million. Total liabilities increased by USD45 million, and accounts payable increased by USD2 million. The cash paid by the company to its suppliers is most likely closest to:

## Wrong Output
Selected C. USD83 million.

## Choices
A. USD67 million.
B. USD79 million.
C. USD83 million.
