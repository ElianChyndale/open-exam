## Prompt
USD/EUR spot rate is 1.2952 and 1-year forward rate is 1.3001. Which statement is most accurate? A. The euro is trading at a forward premium of 49 points. B. The US dollar is trading at a forward premium of 49 points. C. The forward rate is trading at a discount to the spot rate by 0.0049 points.

## Wrong Output
B. The US dollar is trading at a forward premium of 49 points.
