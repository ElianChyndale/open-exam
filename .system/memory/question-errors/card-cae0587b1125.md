## Prompt
Kobe Steel case essay Q11: Discuss the financial risk implications of the post-scandal stock and bond prices with regard to investor confidence.

## Wrong Output
Answered that shareholders and creditors would choose to sell stocks and bonds to avoid more loss. This captured the direction of confidence loss but missed the required financial-risk interpretation: stock price decline, cost of debt increase, investor assessment of future sales/profitability, and governance as only a first step toward restoring confidence.
