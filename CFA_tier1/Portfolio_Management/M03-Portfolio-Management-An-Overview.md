# M03: Portfolio Management: An Overview

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Portfolio perspective, diversification, risk-return trade-off, process, investor types, asset management industry, mutual funds and pooled products.
- **LOS contract**：describe portfolio approach, process steps, investor types, pension plans, asset management industry, mutual funds and other pooled products.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `process_step`, `investor_type`, `pooled_vehicle`, `active_passive`, or `portfolio_context`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

This module is the process map for the whole PM subject. 它把 risk-return math 放进 portfolio management process：planning, execution and feedback。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio perspective | diversification, total portfolio | 单项投资必须放入组合上下文。 |
| Risk-return trade-off | downside protection, modern portfolio theory | 说明组合构建目的。 |
| Process | planning, execution, feedback | 排序并识别 IPS/implementation/rebalancing。 |
| Investor types | individual, institutional, DB/DC pension | 匹配目标和约束。 |
| Industry/products | mutual funds, ETFs, hedge funds, PE | 比较流动性、费用、透明度、监管。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 看到 IPS/objectives/constraints：planning。
2. 看到 asset allocation/security selection/trading：execution。
3. 看到 performance review/rebalancing/monitoring：feedback。
4. 产品题：先比 liquidity, fees, transparency, regulation, strategy。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Formula / rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Process | Planning -> Execution -> Feedback | Core sequence. |
| NAV | `(assets - liabilities) / shares outstanding` | Mutual fund valuation. |
| Active management | seeks alpha, accepts active risk/cost | Strategy classification. |
| Passive management | tracks benchmark, lower cost/tracking objective | Strategy classification. |
| DC vs DB pension | DC risk borne by participant; DB risk borne by sponsor | Investor-type questions. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `process_order` when planning/execution/feedback are mixed.
- Track `vehicle_features` when ETF/mutual fund/hedge fund/private equity characteristics are swapped.
- Track `pension_type` when DB and DC risk bearers are reversed.
- Track `portfolio_context` when a security is judged without investor/portfolio objective.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Portfolio management starts with security selection | It starts with planning and IPS. |
| Active = better | Active means higher active risk/cost; alpha is not guaranteed. |
| Mutual funds and ETFs are identical | Compare trading, pricing, tax, cost and structure. |
| Diversification ensures downside protection | Diversification reduces some risk but does not eliminate market risk. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Process = planning -> execution -> feedback.
- Portfolio perspective first; security selection second.
- DB sponsor bears liability risk; DC participant bears investment risk.
- NAV = net assets / shares.
- Product choice depends on liquidity, fees, transparency and objective.
