# M05: The Behavioral Biases of Individuals

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Behavioral bias categories, cognitive errors, emotional biases, behavioral finance and market behavior.
- **LOS contract**：compare cognitive errors and emotional biases; discuss biases and investment implications; describe how biases can create market characteristics not explained by traditional finance.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `cognitive_vs_emotional`, `belief_perseverance`, `information_processing`, `emotional_bias`, or `guidance`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Behavioral bias questions are diagnosis-plus-guidance tasks. 本模块要先根据行为证据分类，再说明 investment consequence and corrective guidance。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Categories | cognitive errors, emotional biases | 先分大类。 |
| Belief perseverance | conservatism, confirmation, representativeness, illusion of control, hindsight | 识别坚持原信念。 |
| Information processing | anchoring, mental accounting, framing, availability | 识别处理信息的捷径错误。 |
| Emotional biases | loss aversion, overconfidence, self-control, status quo, endowment, regret aversion | 识别情绪驱动。 |
| Guidance | educate, moderate, accommodate | 连接 IPS 和可执行动作。 |
| Market behavior | anomalies, bubbles, momentum, mispricing | 解释传统金融难以解释的现象。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 先从行为证据出发，不从偏差名称猜。
2. Cognitive: often corrected through education, evidence, process and checklist。
3. Emotional: if goals still achievable, may accommodate; if destructive, moderate。
4. Guidance 必须具体：rebalance rule, staged sale, concentration limit, written criteria。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Framework | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bias diagnosis | behavior clue -> bias category -> consequence -> guidance | Full answer structure. |
| Cognitive response | educate/moderate with facts and process | Correctable reasoning errors. |
| Emotional response | accommodate when harmless; moderate when goal-threatening | Emotion-driven behavior. |
| IPS link | bias may distort willingness, allocation, trading, diversification | Suitability. |
| Market link | biased investors can create mispricing/anomalies | Concept application. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `bias_name_trigger` when answer selects a familiar bias without matching behavior.
- Track `cognitive_emotional_flip` when guidance style is wrong.
- Track `overconfidence_vs_illusion_control` when confidence in skill and belief in control are mixed.
- Track `loss_aversion_regret` when fear of loss and fear of regret are not separated.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| All biases should be eliminated | Some emotional biases may be accommodated if objectives remain feasible. |
| Cognitive = harmless | Cognitive errors can seriously distort portfolios. |
| Overconfidence and illusion of control are identical | Overconfidence overestimates ability; illusion of control overestimates influence over outcomes. |
| Bias answer can stop at name | Must include consequence and guidance. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Cognitive = reasoning/information issue; emotional = feeling/attachment issue.
- Belief perseverance holds prior beliefs; information processing uses flawed shortcuts.
- Emotional biases often require accommodation or moderation.
- Always answer: clue -> bias -> consequence -> guidance.
