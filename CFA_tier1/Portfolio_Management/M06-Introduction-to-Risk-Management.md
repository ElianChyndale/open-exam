# M06: Introduction to Risk Management

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Risk management process, framework, governance, tolerance, budgeting, financial vs non-financial risks, interactions, measurement and modification.
- **LOS contract**：define risk management; describe framework, governance, tolerance, budgeting, risk sources/interactions, and measurement/modification methods.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `process_order`, `governance`, `risk_tolerance`, `risk_budgeting`, `risk_type`, or `risk_modification`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Risk management is not risk minimization. 本模块要把 objectives and tolerance 转成 identification, measurement, modification, monitoring and governance。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Process | identify, measure, monitor, modify | 掌握流程顺序和反馈。 |
| Framework | policies, tools, reporting, limits | 说明制度化风险管理。 |
| Governance | board, senior management, accountability, culture | 判断谁负责、如何升级。 |
| Tolerance | willingness/ability to accept risk | 将风险上限转成 limits。 |
| Budgeting | allocate risk to activities/managers | 把 risk tolerance 分配到组合。 |
| Risk types | market, credit, liquidity, operational, model, legal, settlement | 先分类再选工具。 |
| Modification | avoid, prevent/control, transfer, diversify, accept | 选择匹配风险源的动作。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 问 purpose：answer appropriate risk for objectives, not minimum risk。
2. 问 governance：board/senior management set oversight, limits and accountability。
3. 问 risk type：先看 source, not symptom。
4. 问 modification：avoid/prevent/transfer/diversify/accept 要与风险来源匹配。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Framework | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Process | objectives/tolerance -> identify -> measure -> modify -> monitor/communicate | Sequence. |
| Risk tolerance | maximum acceptable risk consistent with objectives | Limit setting. |
| Risk budget | allocation of total risk tolerance across exposures | Governance and manager control. |
| Financial risks | market, credit, liquidity | Asset/portfolio exposures. |
| Non-financial risks | operational, model, legal/regulatory, settlement, reputational | Process/control exposures. |
| Modification choice | avoid/prevent/transfer/diversify/accept | Response selection. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `minimum_risk_misread` when risk management is treated as eliminating risk.
- Track `risk_type_source` when operational/model/legal/market risks are confused.
- Track `risk_response_match` when transfer/avoid/diversify/accept is mismatched.
- Track `governance_role` when board oversight and day-to-day management are blurred.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Risk management = avoid all risk | It supports objectives by taking appropriate compensated risk. |
| Risk tolerance and risk budget are identical | Tolerance is total acceptable risk; budget allocates it. |
| Hedging solves every risk | Some risks are better avoided, controlled, diversified or accepted. |
| Governance is paperwork | Governance defines accountability, limits and escalation. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Objective: align risk with return objective.
- Process: set tolerance -> identify -> measure -> modify -> monitor.
- Governance: accountability, limits, reporting, culture.
- Risk budget allocates tolerance.
- Match response to risk source: avoid, control, transfer, diversify, accept.
