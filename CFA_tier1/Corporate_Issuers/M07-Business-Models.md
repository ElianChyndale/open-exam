# M07: Business Models

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: business model definition, features, pricing and revenue models, value proposition, organization/capabilities, conventional models, variations, innovation, network effects and platforms.
- **C+ output**: 能识别 business model features and infer revenue quality, scalability, margin and capital needs。
- **Evidence anchor**: V3 Module 7, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Business model analysis explains how a company turns customers and capabilities into cash flows. 它连接 Equity 的 company analysis and forecasting，也连接 Corporate Issuers 的 capital needs。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Define the business model.
2. Features: who customers/market, what offering, where channels, how much pricing.
3. Pricing and revenue models.
4. Value proposition: who + what + where + how much.
5. Business organization and capabilities.
6. Types: conventional, variations, innovation, network effects and platform models.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `subscription` -> recurring revenue, retention/churn matters.
- `usage-based` -> revenue varies with customer activity.
- `marketplace/platform` -> network effects, two-sided pricing, scale potential.
- `freemium` -> free users may support conversion or network value.
- `business model innovation` -> new way to create/capture value, not just new product label.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Framework | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Business model features | who, what, where, how much, why value proposition | Answer all before judging quality. |
| Pricing models | one-time, subscription, usage-based, freemium, bundling, razor-and-blade | Pricing affects revenue timing and customer behavior. |
| Revenue models | transaction, recurring, advertising, licensing, marketplace, data/services | Compare stability and scalability. |
| Platform model | network effects and two-sided participation | May subsidize one side to grow the other. |
| Capability fit | resources, processes, organization, technology | Model must be executable, not just attractive. |

## 4.1 Essay Answer Template 作答模板

遇到 business model essay，先完整描述模型，再补 risks / opportunities / questions：

```text
The company's customers are [specific segment], who need [pain point].

Its product creates value by [solving the pain point] through [key differentiation].

Its channels are [channels], which are appropriate because [reason].

Its pricing strategy is [premium/low-cost/bundled/subscription], supported by [reason].

Key risks include [demand risk], [cost risk], and [competitive/regulatory/channel risk].

Key opportunities include [market expansion], [differentiation], and [scale/brand/network effects].

Key questions include whether [core assumption 1], whether [core assumption 2], and whether [core assumption 3].
```

- Feature grid question: use the first four sentences.
- Discuss / evaluate question: use all seven sentences.
- Time pressure: each required sentence gets one concrete evidence point before expanding.

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Revenue model is not the same as pricing model; one describes source/timing, the other price mechanism。
- Recurring revenue can be high quality, but churn and acquisition cost still matter。
- Network effects can be powerful but may require scale before profitability。
- Business model novelty does not guarantee value creation。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Business model = who buys, what is offered, where delivered, how priced, why valuable.
- Subscription = recurring; transaction = event-based; platform = network effects.
- Good model must be profitable, scalable, defensible and fundable.
