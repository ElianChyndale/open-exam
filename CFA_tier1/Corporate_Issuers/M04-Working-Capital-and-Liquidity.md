# M04: Working Capital and Liquidity

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: cash conversion cycle, liquidity, liquidity sources, drags/pulls, measuring and managing working capital and liquidity.
- **C+ output**: 能计算 CCC/liquidity ratios，并解释 liquidity vs profitability trade-off。
- **Evidence anchor**: V3 Module 4, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Working capital is timing. 公司可能 profitable but illiquid if cash is tied up in inventory and receivables. 考试常用 days measures and ratios 来要求比较 issuers。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Cash conversion cycle: inventory days + receivable days - payable days.
2. Liquidity: ability to meet short-term obligations.
3. Primary and secondary liquidity sources.
4. Liquidity drags and pulls.
5. Liquidity ratios and interpretation.
6. Managing inventory, receivables, payables and short-term funding.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `longer DIO/DSO` -> cash tied up longer, CCC increases.
- `longer DPO` -> supplier financing increases, CCC decreases, but supplier relationship risk may rise.
- `current ratio` -> broad liquidity.
- `quick ratio` -> excludes inventory.
- `cash ratio` -> most conservative.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Formula / Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| DIO | `average inventory / COGS x 365` | Inventory holding period. |
| DSO | `average receivables / revenue x 365` | Collection period. |
| DPO | `average payables / purchases or COGS x 365` | Payment period. |
| CCC | `DIO + DSO - DPO` | Shorter usually improves liquidity. |
| Current ratio | `current assets / current liabilities` | Can be inflated by slow inventory. |
| Quick ratio | `(cash + marketable securities + receivables) / current liabilities` | Stricter than current ratio. |
| Cash ratio | `(cash + marketable securities) / current liabilities` | Most conservative. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Review tag: `Corporate-M04-working-capital`.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- CCC subtracts payables days because suppliers finance the operating cycle。
- Higher current ratio is not always better if assets are obsolete or idle。
- Aggressive collections or delayed payments can hurt customers/suppliers。
- Liquidity management balances risk and return; it is not ratio maximization。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Operating cycle = DIO + DSO.
- CCC = DIO + DSO - DPO.
- Quick removes inventory; cash ratio removes receivables too.
- Liquidity means meet obligations on time, not just show accounting profit.
