# M06: Capital Structure

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: WACC, factors affecting capital structure and WACC, Modigliani-Miller propositions, optimal/target capital structure.
- **C+ output**: 能计算 WACC，并解释 leverage, taxes, distress costs, lifecycle and top-down factors。
- **Evidence anchor**: V3 Module 6, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Capital structure asks how a company should finance assets. 债务带来 tax shield but also financial distress and agency costs; equity provides flexibility but may be costlier or dilute owners。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Cost of capital and WACC.
2. Financing needs: capital-intensive vs capital-light, lifecycle, asset tangibility.
3. Cost determinants: top-down and issuer-specific factors.
4. MM propositions without taxes and with taxes.
5. Optimal capital structure, target weights, pecking order and agency costs.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `market value weights available` -> use market weights for WACC.
- `debt cost in WACC` -> after-tax because interest is tax deductible.
- `more debt with taxes` -> tax shield increases firm value before distress costs.
- `more debt without taxes under MM` -> firm value unchanged, cost of equity rises.
- `optimal structure` -> balances tax benefits against distress/agency costs.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Formula / Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| WACC | `wd rd(1 - t) + wp rp + we re` | Use market value target weights. |
| After-tax debt | `rd(1 - t)` | Tax shield applies to interest. |
| MM no tax I | capital structure irrelevant to firm value | Assumes perfect markets. |
| MM no tax II | more debt raises cost of equity | Equity risk rises with leverage. |
| MM with tax | debt tax shield increases firm value | Distress costs eventually offset. |
| Top-down factors | rates, taxes, market conditions, regulation, macro risk | Affect financing costs. |
| Issuer factors | cash-flow stability, collateral, profitability, governance, credit quality | Affect debt capacity and WACC. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Expected item types: WACC calculation, after-tax debt, interpret MM proposition, identify capital structure factor, target structure implication.
- Review tag: `Corporate-M06-capital-structure`.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Use market value weights when possible; book values can be stale。
- Debt is cheaper partly because of priority and tax shield, but too much debt raises risk。
- WACC is for projects with similar risk to the firm; project risk mismatch matters。
- MM propositions depend on assumptions; do not mix no-tax and tax conclusions。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- WACC = weighted cost of debt after tax + preferred + equity.
- More debt: tax shield up, distress risk up, equity risk up.
- Target structure guides weights.
- Optimal structure balances benefits and costs.
