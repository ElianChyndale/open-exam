# M02: Investors and Other Stakeholders

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: lender/shareholder financial claims and motivations, stakeholder groups and interests, ESG factors considered by investors.
- **C+ output**: 能用 claim priority and motivation 解释 stakeholder conflicts。
- **Evidence anchor**: V3 Module 2, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Corporate decisions affect different claimholders differently. 本模块把 debt, equity and non-investor stakeholders 放在同一 conflict map 上。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Debt vs equity claims: contractual priority vs residual ownership.
2. Risk and return: lenders seek downside protection; shareholders seek upside.
3. Conflicts: asset substitution, underinvestment, payout/leverage choices.
4. Stakeholder groups: investors, board, managers, employees, customers, suppliers, governments.
5. ESG: environmental, social and governance risks/opportunities.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `debt holder` -> priority claim, fixed return, covenant protection.
- `shareholder` -> residual claim, voting/control rights, upside exposure.
- `higher leverage or risky project` -> may benefit shareholders while harming creditors.
- `ESG risk` -> can affect cash flows, reputation, regulation and cost of capital.
- `stakeholder conflict` -> name both parties before choosing governance response.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Debt claim | fixed contractual payments and priority in liquidation | Limited upside, lower risk than equity. |
| Equity claim | residual cash flows and control rights | Higher upside and last claim. |
| Shareholder-creditor conflict | shareholders may favor riskier projects or higher payouts | Covenants can limit wealth transfer. |
| Stakeholder mapping | identify interest: returns, wages, product quality, payment terms, tax/regulation | Interests can align or conflict. |
| ESG factor | classify E, S or G and state financial channel | Avoid moralizing without investment effect. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Expected item types: compare debt/equity, identify stakeholder interest, classify ESG factor, diagnose conflict.
- Review tag: `Corporate-M02-stakeholders`.
- Miss log fields: stakeholder pair, claim priority, motivation, conflict channel, ESG category.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Debt is safer than equity because of priority, not because it has no risk。
- Shareholders and creditors can both provide capital but have different incentives。
- ESG is investment-relevant when it affects risk, cash flows, regulation or valuation。
- Board and managers are not always the same stakeholder group。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Debt = contract + priority + capped upside.
- Equity = residual + control + upside.
- Conflict answer always names who gains, who bears risk, and what mechanism can help.
