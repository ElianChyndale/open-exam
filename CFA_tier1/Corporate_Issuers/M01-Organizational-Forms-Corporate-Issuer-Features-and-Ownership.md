# M01: Organizational Forms, Corporate Issuer Features, and Ownership

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: organizational forms, key corporate issuer features, public vs privately owned issuers.
- **C+ output**: 能把 legal form 转成 liability, tax, capital access, control and disclosure implications。
- **Evidence anchor**: V3 Module 1, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

This module defines the issuer before analyzing its stakeholders or financing. 组织形式决定谁承担 liability、谁控制 business、公司能否持续存在并吸引 external capital。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Organizational forms: sole trader/proprietorship, partnerships, limited companies.
2. Corporate issuer features: separate legal identity, owner-manager separation, limited liability, external financing, taxation.
3. Public vs private: exchange listing, liquidity, price transparency, share issuance, disclosure, going public/private.
4. Corporate owners: founders/families, strategic owners, institutional investors, governments, employees.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `limited liability` -> owners' losses generally limited to invested capital.
- `separate legal identity` -> company can own assets, contract, sue/be sued.
- `owner-manager separation` -> enables scale but creates agency problems.
- `publicly listed` -> liquidity, price transparency and disclosure obligations.
- `private issuer` -> less disclosure and liquidity; ownership often concentrated.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader | easy formation/control, unlimited liability, limited capital access | Owner and business closely linked. |
| Partnership | shared ownership/resources, often pass-through style, partner liability varies | General vs limited partner matters. |
| Limited company | separate legal identity, limited liability, easier external capital | Agency and disclosure issues emerge. |
| Public issuer | listed shares, liquid secondary market, more disclosure | Public market access raises transparency demands. |
| Private issuer | negotiated ownership, less liquid, fewer public disclosures | Valuation and exit routes are less observable. |

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Limited liability protects owners, not necessarily managers from misconduct。
- Public ownership improves liquidity but increases regulatory and disclosure requirements。
- Owner-manager separation is both a scaling benefit and agency conflict source。
- Going private reduces public disclosure but does not remove business risk。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Legal form drives liability, tax, continuity and capital access.
- Corporation = separate legal person + limited liability + external financing.
- Public = liquid/transparent/disclosed; private = concentrated/negotiated/illiquid.
