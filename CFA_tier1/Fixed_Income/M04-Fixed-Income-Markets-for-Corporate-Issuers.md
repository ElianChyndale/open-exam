# M04: Fixed-Income Markets for Corporate Issuers

> **Module job**：识别 corporations and financial institutions 如何用 short-term funding, repo, commercial paper, loans, and long-term debt 融资。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：compare short-term funding alternatives；describe repos, uses, benefits, and risks；contrast long-term funding of investment-grade versus high-yield corporate issuers。
- **Textbook anchors**：External Loan Financing；Lines of Credit；Secured Loans and Factoring；Commercial Paper；Repurchase Agreements；Long-Term Corporate Debt；IG vs HY Issuance。
- **Output standard**：遇到 corporate funding 题，先判断 tenor, collateral, issuer quality, and market access。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M04 把公司发行人的融资工具和信用质量连接起来。它既为 credit analysis 打基础，也解释为什么不同 issuer 会选择 loan, CP, repo, or bond market。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Short-term funding**：bank loans, lines of credit, factoring, commercial paper, deposits/interbank for financial institutions。
2. **Repo**：sale plus repurchase agreement, economically collateralized borrowing。
3. **Repo risk**：counterparty, collateral, liquidity, margin/haircut risk。
4. **Long-term debt**：IG issuers have broader access and lower spreads; HY issuers face tighter covenants and higher required yields。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If funding is collateralized by receivables, think factoring or secured loan。
- If high-quality short-term unsecured paper appears, think commercial paper。
- If transaction is sell security now and buy back later, think repo financing。
- If comparing IG vs HY, use credit quality, covenants, spread, and market access。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Repo | Borrower sells collateral and repurchases later |
| Haircut | More risk or lower collateral quality -> higher haircut |
| Commercial paper | Short-term unsecured funding for strong issuers |
| Line of credit | Bank commitment, useful liquidity backstop |
| Factoring | Receivables converted into cash |
| IG vs HY | IG lower spread and lighter covenant pressure; HY higher spread and more investor protection |

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Repo is collateralized borrowing in economic substance, even though legal form is sale/repurchase。
- Commercial paper is typically for high-quality issuers, not distressed funding。
- Higher haircut protects lender, not borrower。
- HY bonds are not defined by maturity; they are defined by credit quality。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Corporate funding choice = tenor + credit quality + collateral + market access。
- Repo = collateralized short-term borrowing。
- IG = cheaper/broader access；HY = higher spread/tighter protections。
