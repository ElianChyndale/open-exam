# M17: Fixed-Income Securitization

> **Module job**：解释 securitization 如何把 asset pool cash flows 通过 SPE 转给 investors，并说明 issuer, investor, economy, and market benefits。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：explain benefits of securitization for issuers, investors, economies, and financial markets；describe securitization, including parties and roles。
- **Textbook anchors**：Benefits to Issuers；Benefits to Investors；Benefits to Economies and Financial Markets；The Securitization Process；Example of Securitization；Parties；Role of the SPE。
- **Output standard**：能画出 originator -> asset pool -> SPE -> securities -> investors 的现金流和风险转移。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M17 是证券化入口。它不要求复杂 MBS 计算，但必须把 SPE, bankruptcy remoteness, servicing, credit enhancement, and cash-flow transfer 讲清楚。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Why securitize**：funding diversification, balance sheet management, liquidity, investor access。
2. **Investor benefit**：access to asset pools, tailored risk/return, diversification。
3. **Market benefit**：more funding channels and risk transfer。
4. **Process**：originator sells assets to SPE; SPE issues securities backed by asset cash flows。
5. **Parties**：originator, seller/servicer, SPE, investors, trustee, credit enhancer, underwriter。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If assets are isolated from originator bankruptcy, think SPE and bankruptcy remoteness。
- If question asks benefits, separate issuer, investor, and economy/market。
- If role question appears, map each party to cash-flow collection, issuance, oversight, or support。
- If risk transfer appears, remember not all risk disappears; it is redistributed。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Securitization component | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Originator | Creates/sells asset pool |
| SPE/SPV | Holds assets and issues securities |
| Servicer | Collects payments and manages assets |
| Trustee | Protects investor interests and monitors compliance |
| Credit enhancement | Improves credit profile through support or structure |
| Bankruptcy remoteness | Separates asset pool from originator default risk |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 练习与模考证据

- **EOC anchor**：`CFA2447-eorq-q.xhtml` / `CFA2447-eorq-a.xhtml`。
- **Evidence to capture**：SPE role missed；servicer/trustee confused；benefit assigned to wrong party；risk elimination overstated。
- **Review tag**：`Fixed Income / securitization / SPE process parties`。

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Securitization redistributes risk; it does not eliminate risk。
- SPE holds assets and issues securities; servicer collects payments。
- Bankruptcy remoteness is about originator risk separation。
- Investor still faces asset pool performance and structural risk。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Pool assets -> SPE -> issue securities -> cash flows to investors。
- SPE = isolation and issuance vehicle。
- Benefits differ by issuer, investor, and market。
