# M03: Fixed-Income Issuance and Trading

> **Module job**：把 fixed-income market 看成 segmented, dealer-driven, and issuer-specific market。核心是 market segments, indexes, primary issuance, and secondary trading。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：describe fixed-income market segments and issuer/investor participants；describe types of fixed-income indexes；compare primary and secondary fixed-income markets to equity markets。
- **Textbook anchors**：Fixed-Income Segments, Issuers, and Investors；Fixed-Income Indexes；Primary and Secondary Fixed-Income Markets。
- **Output standard**：能解释 bond market 为什么比 equity market 更分散、更依赖 dealer and OTC trading。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M03 是市场地图。它为 M04-M05 的 issuer markets 以及 M06 的 pricing liquidity context 铺路。考试偏概念比较，不是重计算。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Segments**：issuer type, maturity, credit quality, currency, coupon type, securitization type。
2. **Participants**：issuers seek funding; investors seek income, diversification, matching liabilities。
3. **Indexes**：fixed-income indexes are broader and harder to replicate because bonds are numerous and less liquid。
4. **Primary vs secondary**：primary market raises capital; secondary market provides liquidity and price discovery。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If asked **bond vs equity market**：bond market has more securities per issuer and more OTC/dealer trading。
- If asked **index construction**：think maturity, credit, issuer, currency, and rebalancing/turnover。
- If asked **primary issuance**：issuer receives proceeds。
- If asked **secondary trading**：investor trades with another investor/dealer; issuer usually does not receive funds。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Concept | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Primary market | New securities issued, capital raised |
| Secondary market | Existing securities traded, liquidity/price discovery |
| Dealer market | Dealer quotes bid/ask and holds inventory risk |
| Index representativeness | More issues, turnover, and illiquidity make replication harder |
| Segment classification | Always classify by issuer, maturity, credit, currency, coupon, collateral |

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Secondary trading does not normally fund the issuer。
- Bond indexes can be difficult to replicate because bonds mature, default, are called, or become illiquid。
- A single corporate issuer can have many bonds with different maturities and seniority。
- OTC market does not mean unregulated; it means trade negotiation is decentralized。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Primary = new issue funding；Secondary = liquidity。
- Bond market = many issues, OTC, dealer, less transparent than exchange equities。
- Index questions = inclusion rules + liquidity + turnover。
