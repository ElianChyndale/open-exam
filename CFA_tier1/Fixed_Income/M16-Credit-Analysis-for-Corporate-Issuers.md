# M16: Credit Analysis for Corporate Issuers

> **Module job**：把 corporate creditworthiness 拆成 business risk, financial risk, ratios, liquidity, seniority, security, recovery, and issuer/issue ratings。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：describe qualitative and quantitative factors for corporate creditworthiness；calculate and interpret credit ratios；describe seniority rankings, secured vs unsecured debt, bankruptcy priority, and impact on ratings。
- **Textbook anchors**：Assessing Corporate Creditworthiness；Qualitative Factors；Quantitative Factors；Financial Ratios；Seniority Rankings；Secured versus Unsecured Debt；Recovery Rates；Issuer and Issue Ratings。
- **Output standard**：遇到 corporate bond, 先判断 issuer strength，再判断 specific issue priority and collateral。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M16 是固定收益和 FSA/Corporate Issuers 的接口。考试既会问 ratio direction，也会问同一 issuer 不同债项为何 issue rating 不同。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Qualitative factors**：industry structure, competitive position, management, governance。
2. **Quantitative factors**：profitability, leverage, coverage, cash flow, liquidity。
3. **Credit ratios**：higher leverage worsens credit; higher coverage/liquidity improves credit。
4. **Seniority and security**：secured/senior claims recover before subordinated/unsecured claims。
5. **Issuer vs issue rating**：issue-specific collateral/seniority can lift or lower issue rating relative to issuer rating。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If ratio has debt in numerator, higher usually weaker credit。
- If ratio has earnings/cash flow over interest/debt service, higher usually stronger credit。
- If secured senior debt appears, expect higher recovery than unsecured/subordinated。
- If issuer and issue ratings differ, look for collateral, seniority, structural subordination, or guarantees。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Ratio / concept | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Debt / EBITDA | Lower is stronger credit |
| Debt / capital | Lower leverage is stronger |
| EBITDA / interest | Higher coverage is stronger |
| CFO / debt | Higher cash-flow coverage is stronger |
| FCF after capex/dividends | Positive recurring FCF supports debt repayment |
| Senior secured debt | Higher priority and collateral, often higher recovery |
| Subordinated debt | Lower priority, lower expected recovery |

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Higher leverage ratio is not better just because company is larger。
- Issuer rating reflects obligor; issue rating reflects specific instrument features。
- Secured debt has claim on collateral; unsecured relies on general credit。
- Recovery ranking matters after default, not necessarily before default coupon timing。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Corporate credit = business risk + financial risk + debt structure。
- Coverage/liquidity up = stronger；leverage up = weaker。
- Senior secured > senior unsecured > subordinated in recovery priority。
