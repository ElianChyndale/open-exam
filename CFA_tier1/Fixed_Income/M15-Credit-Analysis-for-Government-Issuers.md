# M15: Credit Analysis for Government Issuers

> **Module job**：把 government credit 拆成 sovereign and non-sovereign issuer analysis。核心是 ability and willingness to pay, currency of debt, fiscal/monetary flexibility, and support mechanism。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：explain special considerations when evaluating credit of sovereign and non-sovereign government debt issuers and issues。
- **Textbook anchors**：Sovereign Credit Analysis；Qualitative Factors；Quantitative Factors；Non-Sovereign Government Debt；Agencies；Government Sector Banks；Supranational Issuers；Regional Government Issuers；General Obligation Bonds；Revenue Bonds。
- **Output standard**：能区分 sovereign, agency, regional, and revenue-backed debt 的 repayment source。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M15 是 M05 issuer map 的 credit version。政府信用不是简单等于无风险，尤其是 foreign-currency debt, regional debt, agency debt, and revenue bonds。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Sovereign qualitative factors**：institutional strength, political stability, policy credibility。
2. **Sovereign quantitative factors**：GDP growth, debt/GDP, fiscal balance, external debt, reserves, inflation。
3. **Currency distinction**：local-currency and foreign-currency debt have different risk。
4. **Non-sovereign issuers**：agency support, regional tax authority, project revenues, supranational support。
5. **GO vs revenue bonds**：general obligation backed by taxing power; revenue bonds backed by project revenue。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If sovereign debt is foreign currency, focus on external reserves and hard-currency access。
- If agency debt appears, check explicit guarantee。
- If regional debt appears, check taxing power and intergovernmental support。
- If revenue bond appears, analyze project revenue rather than general tax base。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Credit lens | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Ability to pay | Economic strength, fiscal position, debt burden, external liquidity |
| Willingness to pay | Political/institutional stability and policy credibility |
| Local currency debt | Monetary flexibility may reduce default risk but inflation/devaluation risk remains |
| Foreign currency debt | External reserves and FX access matter more |
| General obligation | Broad taxing authority supports repayment |
| Revenue bond | Specific project revenue supports repayment |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 练习与模考证据

- **EOC anchor**：`CFA2445-eorq-q.xhtml` / `CFA2445-eorq-a.xhtml`。
- **Evidence to capture**：local/foreign currency ignored；GO/revenue bond confused；agency guarantee assumed；sovereign credit treated as risk-free。
- **Review tag**：`Fixed Income / government credit analysis`。

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Sovereign issuer can default, especially on foreign-currency debt。
- Explicit support is stronger than implicit support。
- Revenue bond repayment depends on project revenue, not full taxing power。
- Supranational risk depends on member support and institutional structure。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Government credit = ability + willingness + currency + support source。
- GO = tax backing；Revenue = project cash flow。
- Agency support must be evidenced, not assumed。
