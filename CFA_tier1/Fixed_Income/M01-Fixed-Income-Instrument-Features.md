# M01: Fixed-Income Instrument Features

> **Module job**：把债券先看成 contract, not formula。先识别 issuer, maturity, par, coupon, seniority, covenants, and embedded provisions，再进入后续估值和风险模块。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：describe the features of a fixed-income security；describe the contents of a bond indenture and contrast affirmative and negative covenants。
- **Textbook anchors**：Features of Fixed-Income Securities；Issuer；Maturity；Principal；Coupon Rate and Frequency；Seniority；Contingency Provisions；Yield Measures；Yield Curves；Bond Indentures and Covenants。
- **Output standard**：看到一只债，能先画出 contract facts，再判断哪些条款保护 issuer, investor, or lender。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M01 是全科入口。它不要求大量计算，但决定后面每一道 pricing, duration, credit, securitization 题的输入是否读对。核心问题是：谁发行、何时还本、怎么付息、谁优先索偿、契约限制什么。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Security features**：issuer, maturity, principal, coupon rate/frequency, currency, seniority。
2. **Contingency provisions**：call, put, conversion and sinking fund change cash-flow uncertainty。
3. **Indenture**：legal contract between issuer and bondholders, with trustee and covenants。
4. **Covenants**：affirmative covenants require actions; negative covenants restrict actions。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If question asks **feature identification**：match term to cash-flow or legal meaning。
- If question asks **investor protection**：look for seniority, collateral, restrictive covenant, put feature。
- If question asks **issuer flexibility**：look for call feature, negative pledge limits, refunding ability。
- If question asks **credit implication**：contract terms affect recovery and spread, not only coupon size。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Decision point | Use this rule |
|---|---|
| Coupon frequency | More frequent coupons change periodic yield conversion later |
| Seniority | Higher seniority generally higher recovery, lower spread |
| Callable bond | Benefits issuer; investor demands higher yield or spread |
| Putable bond | Benefits investor; investor accepts lower yield |
| Convertible bond | Investor gets equity upside; coupon/yield may be lower |
| Affirmative covenant | Issuer must do something, such as pay taxes or maintain assets |
| Negative covenant | Issuer must not do something, such as issue more senior debt |

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Do not treat high coupon as automatically lower credit risk。
- Call protects issuer, put protects investor。
- Maturity is final repayment date, not duration。
- Seniority affects recovery ranking, not timing of coupon payments under normal conditions。
- Negative covenant is a restriction, not a bad covenant。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Bond = promised cash flows + legal contract + priority ranking。
- Indenture = legal terms; covenants = action rules。
- Affirmative = must do；Negative = must not do。
- Investor-friendly provisions usually lower required yield; issuer-friendly provisions usually raise required yield。
