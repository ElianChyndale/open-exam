# M05: Fixed-Income Markets for Government Issuers

> **Module job**：区分 sovereign, non-sovereign, quasi-government, agency, municipal/regional, and supranational debt，并比较 government and corporate issuance/trading。

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official LOS**：describe funding choices by sovereign and non-sovereign governments, quasi-government entities, and supranational agencies；contrast issuance and trading of government and corporate instruments。
- **Textbook anchors**：Sovereign Debt；Sovereign Debt Issuance and Trading；Government Agencies；Local and Regional Government Authorities；Supranational Organizations。
- **Output standard**：能识别 issuer type and backing source, then infer liquidity, credit, and benchmark role。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M05 给固定收益建立 risk-free benchmark and public-sector issuer map。后续 spot/par/forward curves, spread measures, and sovereign credit analysis 都依赖这个基础。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Sovereign debt**：central government debt, often benchmark curve in domestic currency。
2. **Issuance/trading**：auctions and active secondary markets for major sovereigns。
3. **Agencies/quasi-government**：may have explicit or implicit support, but not always full sovereign guarantee。
4. **Local/regional/municipal**：credit depends on taxing authority, revenue sources, and legal framework。
5. **Supranational**：multilateral issuers funded by member support and capital markets。

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

- If debt is issued by national government, ask domestic vs foreign currency。
- If agency appears, check whether guarantee is explicit。
- If municipal/regional appears, distinguish general tax backing from project revenue backing。
- If comparing with corporate debt, government debt usually has different benchmark, liquidity, and credit drivers。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Issuer | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Sovereign | Monetary/fiscal power matters; often benchmark yield curve |
| Agency/quasi-government | Support may be explicit, implicit, or absent |
| Municipal/regional | Tax authority and revenue source drive credit |
| Supranational | Member support and preferred creditor status may matter |
| Government vs corporate | Government often more liquid and benchmark-linked; corporate spread reflects issuer credit |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 练习与模考证据

- **EOC anchor**：`CFA2435-eorq-q.xhtml` / `CFA2435-eorq-a.xhtml`。
- **Evidence to capture**：agency guarantee assumed without evidence；municipal revenue source ignored；government/corporate benchmark role confused。
- **Review tag**：`Fixed Income / government issuer markets / public debt`。

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

- Agency debt is not automatically sovereign debt。
- Domestic-currency sovereign risk differs from foreign-currency sovereign risk。
- Government bonds can still have credit risk, liquidity risk, and inflation risk。
- Supranational issuer is not the same as a national government。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终速记

- Government issuer map = sovereign -> agency/quasi -> local/regional -> supranational。
- Always ask: who backs the debt and with what source of repayment?
- Sovereign benchmark curve later anchors spread and term-structure questions。
