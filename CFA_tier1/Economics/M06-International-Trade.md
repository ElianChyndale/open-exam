# M06: International Trade

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Benefits/costs of trade, tariffs, quotas, export subsidies, trading blocs and regional integration.
- **LOS contract**：describe trade benefits/costs; compare tariffs, quotas and export subsidies; explain motivations and advantages of trading blocs, common markets and economic unions.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `comparative_advantage`, `trade_restriction`, `terms_of_trade`, or `regional_integration`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

International trade questions ask who gains, who loses, and what restriction changes price, quantity, welfare and government revenue/rents. 本模块从 comparative advantage 进入，再比较 tariff/quota/subsidy and blocs。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Benefits/costs | specialization, comparative advantage, distributional effects | 区分总收益和分配损失。 |
| Tariffs | import tax, revenue, higher domestic price | 判断政府收入和福利损失。 |
| Quotas | quantity limit, quota rent | 判断数量限制和租金归属。 |
| Export subsidies | subsidy to exporters | 判断出口增加但可能降低 national welfare。 |
| Trading blocs | FTA, customs union, common market, economic union | 按 integration depth 排序。 |
| Trade creation/diversion | lower-cost member vs higher-cost member replacing world supplier | 判断 bloc 是否提高效率。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 比较优势题：不要看谁产出多，先算 opportunity cost。
2. Tariff vs quota：tariff 有 government revenue；quota 有 quota rent。
3. Subsidy：出口商受益不等于国家整体福利一定受益。
4. Bloc 题：按自由化深度排序，再判断 trade creation/diversion。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Formula / rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity cost | `OC(A) = units B forgone / units A gained` | Comparative advantage. |
| Terms of trade | `TOT = export price index / import price index` | Higher TOT generally favorable. |
| Tariff | domestic price rises; imports fall; government revenue | Restriction comparison. |
| Quota | import quantity capped; quota rents | Restriction comparison. |
| Export subsidy | export supply increases; fiscal cost; possible welfare loss | Policy impact. |
| Integration order | FTA -> customs union -> common market -> economic union/monetary union | Trading bloc classification. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `absolute_vs_comparative` when efficient producer is selected without OC.
- Track `tariff_quota_revenue` when tariff revenue and quota rent are mixed.
- Track `subsidy_welfare` when exporter gain is treated as national welfare gain.
- Track `bloc_order` when common market/customs union/economic union are reversed.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Absolute advantage decides trade | Comparative advantage decides specialization. |
| Tariff and quota are identical | Tariff creates government revenue; quota creates rents. |
| Export subsidy always helps home country | It can reduce national welfare despite helping exporters. |
| FTA equals customs union | Customs union adds common external tariff. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Comparative advantage = lowest opportunity cost.
- `TOT = export price index / import price index`.
- Tariff: price up, imports down, government revenue.
- Quota: quantity cap, quota rent.
- Bloc depth: FTA -> customs union -> common market -> economic union/monetary union.
