# M01: The Firm and Market Structures

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Profit maximization, breakeven, shutdown, economies of scale, market structures, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, concentration measures.
- **LOS contract**：determine breakeven/shutdown; describe perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, monopoly; explain pricing/output under monopolistic competition and oligopoly; identify market structure and concentration-measure limits.
- **Evidence rule**：所有错题必须标出是 `cost condition`, `market structure classification`, `oligopoly strategy`, 还是 `concentration measure`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

本模块是 Economics 的 micro foundation：先用成本曲线判断 firm 是否生产，再用 barriers, seller count, differentiation, pricing power 判断市场结构。考试不是让你背四类市场，而是让你把题干特征翻译成 quantity, price, profit, and long-run equilibrium。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Breakeven / shutdown | `P = ATC`, `P < AVC`, `MR = MC` | 区分 break even, continue, shut down, exit。 |
| Economies of scale | `LRAC`, `minimum efficient scale`, `natural monopoly` | 判断长期平均成本下降/上升和市场集中度来源。 |
| Market structures | sellers, product, entry barriers, pricing power | 从特征组合识别四种市场。 |
| Monopolistic competition | differentiated products, low barriers, long-run zero economic profit | 短期可盈利，长期需求曲线与 ATC 相切。 |
| Oligopoly | strategic interdependence, Cournot, Nash, collusion | 先判断是否合谋，再选 Nash/联合利润逻辑。 |
| Concentration | `CR_n`, `HHI` | 计算集中度并说明局限。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 题干给 price and cost：先判 `P` 与 `AVC/ATC`，不要直接说亏损就关停。
2. 题干给 industry description：按 sellers -> product differentiation -> barriers -> pricing power 顺序分类。
3. 题干给 oligopoly payoff：先圈 `collusion`, `side payment`, `Cournot`, `no collusion`。
4. 题干给 concentration：统一 market share 单位，再算 CR 或 HHI，最后写 limitation。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Formula / rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | `TR = P x Q` | Profit calculation base. |
| Accounting profit | `TR - explicit costs` | 会计口径。 |
| Economic profit | `TR - explicit costs - implicit costs` | 经济利润，含机会成本。 |
| Profit max | `MR = MC` | Quantity decision. |
| Breakeven | `P = ATC` | Economic profit = 0. |
| Shutdown | continue if `P >= AVC`; shut down if `P < AVC` | Short-run operating decision. |
| CR | `CR_n = sum largest n shares` | Concentration, not full competition proof. |
| HHI | `sum share_i^2` | Percent share monopoly = `10,000`; decimal share monopoly = `1.0`。 |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `shutdown_vs_exit` when the wrong answer treats economic loss as immediate shutdown.
- Track `barrier_misread` when monopolistic competition is confused with oligopoly because both have differentiation.
- Track `oligopoly_game` when Nash/collusive outcome is chosen without checking the question condition.
- Track `hhi_unit` when percentages and decimals are mixed.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Economic profit = 0 被当成亏损 | Zero economic profit includes normal return to capital. |
| `P < ATC` 就 shutdown | Short-run shutdown uses `P < AVC`; long-run exit uses `P < ATC` persistently. |
| Product differentiation = high barriers | Monopolistic competition has differentiation but low barriers. |
| Cournot 被当作 price competition | Cournot assumes firms choose output and expect rivals' output unchanged. |
| HHI/CR 被当作最终结论 | Concentration measures miss imports, potential entry, and product differences. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- `MR = MC`; then read price from demand/market rule.
- Short run: `P >= AVC` continue; `P < AVC` shut down.
- Long run: `P = ATC` breakeven; `P < ATC` exit pressure.
- Perfect competition = price taker; monopoly = one seller; monopolistic competition = many differentiated low barriers; oligopoly = few + strategic.
- CR and HHI describe concentration, not automatically market power.
