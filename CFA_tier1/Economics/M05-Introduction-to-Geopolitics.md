# M05: Introduction to Geopolitics

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：State/non-state actors, cooperation, globalization, trade organizations, geopolitical risk, geopolitical tools, investment process.
- **LOS contract**：describe cooperation vs competition, globalization, international organizations, geopolitical risk, tools and investment impact.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `actor`, `cooperation`, `risk_type`, `tool`, or `investment_channel`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Geopolitics turns political power and cooperation/competition into economic and investment risk. 本模块要从 actor and motive 出发，再判断 risk type, policy tool, and asset channel。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Actors | state, non-state, institutions, firms | 识别谁在行动。 |
| Cooperation | security, economic interest, resources, standardization, soft power | 判断合作收益与成本。 |
| Globalization | profit, market access, resources, rollback threats | 连接全球化收益和脆弱性。 |
| Organizations | IMF, World Bank, WTO | 识别机构职能。 |
| Risk assessment | event, thematic, exogenous | 用日期/持续性/可预测性分类。 |
| Tools | sanctions, tariffs, capital restrictions, military, cyber, diplomacy | 判断对区域和经济的影响。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 先圈 actor：government, institution, company, non-state group。
2. 再圈 motive：security, economic interest, resource, soft power。
3. 风险分类先看是否有 set date；没有日期再看是否持续主题或外生冲击。
4. 投资题必须说 channel：commodity, supply chain, risk premium, capital flow, FX。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Framework | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cooperation lens | benefits of cooperation vs sovereignty/security/economic cost | Explain cooperation or rollback. |
| Risk taxonomy | event = date-driven; thematic = persistent; exogenous = unexpected external | Classification questions. |
| Investment channel | shock -> cash flow/discount rate/risk premium/liquidity/FX | Asset-price interpretation. |
| Scenario analysis | base/upside/downside paths, not single forecast | Geopolitical risk process. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `event_thematic_exogenous` when risk category is chosen by emotional salience instead of date/predictability.
- Track `institution_role` when IMF, World Bank and WTO functions are mixed.
- Track `channel_missing` when answer identifies geopolitical event but not investment impact.
- Track `actor_motive` when non-state actor or soft power is ignored.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Big event = event risk | Event risk requires a date-driven milestone. |
| Geopolitical risk only means war | It includes sanctions, institutions, cyber, capital controls and trade tools. |
| Globalization only lowers cost | It can raise interdependence and rollback risk. |
| Investment impact is always negative | Some assets/countries/sectors may benefit from shocks. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Actor -> motive -> cooperation/competition -> tool -> investment channel.
- Event risk has date; thematic persists; exogenous is unexpected external.
- Channels: supply chain, commodity, risk premium, capital flows, FX.
- Use scenario analysis rather than point prediction.
