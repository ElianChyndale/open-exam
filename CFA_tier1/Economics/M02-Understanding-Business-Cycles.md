# M02: Understanding Business Cycles

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Business-cycle phases, credit cycles, economic indicators, surveys, big data, nowcasting.
- **LOS contract**：describe business cycle phases, credit cycles, and how resource use, consumer/business activity, housing, external trade and indicators vary over the cycle.
- **Evidence rule**：错题记录必须标 `phase`, `indicator_timing`, `credit_cycle`, or `variable_behavior`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Business cycle questions are time-order questions. 本模块重点不是背 recession/expansion 的中文定义，而是判断题干问的是 current state, future direction, or past confirmation。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Phases | recovery, expansion, slowdown, contraction, trough | 判断经济所处阶段与变量方向。 |
| Leads and lags | orders, inventories, labor, capex | 区分先变和后变。 |
| Credit cycle | lending standards, credit availability, defaults | 将信贷收缩/扩张连接到实体经济。 |
| Indicators | leading, coincident, lagging, composite | 看指标用途，不只看名称。 |
| Nowcasting | high-frequency data, GDPNow | 理解实时估计不是长期预测保证。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 问 future direction：优先 leading indicators。
2. 问 current condition：coincident indicators 更直接。
3. 问 confirmation of past recession：lagging indicators 合理。
4. 出现 mixed signals：先给每个指标贴 timing label，再回答题目问法。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Framework | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle order | expansion -> peak -> contraction -> trough -> recovery | Phase map. |
| Indicator timing | leading / coincident / lagging | Direction vs confirmation. |
| Diffusion index | share of components rising | Breadth of expansion/decline. |
| Credit channel | credit availability -> spending/investment -> output/employment | Amplification mechanism. |
| Productivity trap | output per hour can rise near recession bottom | Lean labor force can lift productivity. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `leading_lagging_flip` when a lagging series is used as a forecast.
- Track `phase_sequence` when recovery, expansion, slowdown, and contraction are mixed.
- Track `productivity_trap` when total output/employment is confused with output per hour.
- Track `credit_cycle_channel` when lending standards/default risk are not linked to output.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Leading indicator = current confirmation | Leading predicts; coincident describes current; lagging confirms past. |
| High unemployment always means future weakness | Unemployment is often lagging; check forward-looking data. |
| Productivity must be highest at boom peak | Lean production near trough can raise productivity. |
| Nowcasting = long-term forecast | Nowcasting estimates current activity using recent data. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Label the question: future/current/past.
- Leading: orders, permits, equity prices, sentiment-type forward signals.
- Coincident: current production/income/sales style measures.
- Lagging: unemployment duration, inflation, delayed credit stress measures.
- Credit cycle magnifies the business cycle through lending and balance sheets.
