# M04: Monetary Policy

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official spine**：Central bank roles, policy objectives, tools, transmission mechanism, inflation/exchange-rate targeting, limitations, interaction with fiscal policy.
- **LOS contract**：describe central bank roles/objectives; describe tools/transmission and relationships with growth, inflation, interest and FX; describe effective central bank qualities, targets and limitations; explain fiscal-monetary interaction.
- **Evidence rule**：错题标 `tool`, `transmission_layer`, `policy_stance`, `inflation_targeting`, or `limitation`。

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Monetary policy is central-bank action through rates, reserves, open market operations and balance-sheet tools. 本模块要把 tool -> channel -> AD/output -> inflation 的链条说完整。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

| Spine | English trigger | 中文任务 |
|---|---|---|
| Central bank roles | price stability, lender of last resort, payments | 识别央行目标和职责。 |
| Tools | OMO, policy rate, reserve requirement, QE | 判断工具方向。 |
| Transmission | interest, credit, asset price, FX channels | 分层说明中间影响和最终影响。 |
| Objectives | inflation targeting, credibility, transparency | 连接预期管理。 |
| Limitations | ZLB, liquidity trap, impaired banks, lags | 判断政策为何失灵。 |
| Policy mix | fiscal plus monetary | 判断组合对 growth/inflation/rates 的影响。 |

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

1. 问 tool：选 central-bank instrument，不选税收/支出。
2. 问 current stance：比较 current policy rate 与 neutral rate。
3. 问 ultimately affects：通常要走到 inflation/price level，不停在 AD。
4. 问 limitation：检查 zero lower bound、liquidity trap、bank lending channel 和 credibility。

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Item | Formula / rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Approx neutral nominal rate | real trend/neutral rate + expected inflation | Policy stance comparison. |
| Expansionary stance | policy rate below neutral; reserves/liquidity up | Stimulates credit, AD and inflation pressure. |
| Contractionary stance | policy rate above neutral; liquidity tight | Slows credit, AD and inflation pressure. |
| Transmission chain | tool -> rates/reserves -> credit/assets/FX -> AD/output -> inflation | Required verbal framework. |
| QE | central bank asset purchases expand reserves/balance sheet | Most relevant when conventional rates constrained but transmission can still work. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Track `ultimate_vs_intermediate` when total demand is selected as the final effect.
- Track `neutral_rate_compare` when future action is confused with current stance.
- Track `tool_actor` when fiscal and monetary tools are mixed.
- Track `qe_liquidity_trap` when every weak economy scenario is assumed to make QE effective.

## 6. Trap Ledger 陷阱账本

| Trap | Fix rule |
|---|---|
| Policy stance asks what central bank should do next | It asks current rate relative to neutral rate. |
| Monetary easing always increases lending | Bank willingness and borrower demand may block transmission. |
| AD is the ultimate effect | AD/output are intermediate; inflation is often ultimate. |
| Central bank credibility is decorative | Credibility anchors expectations and policy effectiveness. |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 最终回忆单

- Monetary actor = central bank.
- Tools = policy rate, OMO, reserves, asset purchases.
- Transmission = rates/credit/assets/FX -> AD/output -> inflation.
- Stance = current policy rate vs neutral rate.
- Limitations = ZLB/liquidity trap/banking channel/lags/credibility.
