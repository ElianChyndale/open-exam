### 例题: 百分位数、四分位数与中位数计算

**材料**: 基于 100 家公司的市值分析（见下表）

| Bin | 累计百分比 | 市值下限 (€B) | 市值上限 (€B) | 观察数 |
|-----|-----------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 5% | 0.28 | 15.45 | 5 |
| 2 | 10% | 15.45 | 21.22 | 5 |
| 3 | 15% | 21.22 | 29.37 | 5 |
| 4 | 20% | 29.37 | 32.57 | 5 |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 20 | 100% | 81.62 | 96.85 | 5 |

**Q1**: The tenth percentile corresponds to observations in bin(s):
- A. 2
- B. 1 and 2
- C. 19 and 20

**Q2**: The second quintile corresponds to observations in bin(s):
- A. 8
- B. 5, 6, 7, and 8
- C. 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10

**Q3**: The fourth quartile corresponds to observations in bin(s):
- A. 17
- B. 17, 18, 19, and 20
- C. 16, 17, 18, 19, and 20

**Q4**: The median is closest to:
- A. 44.86
- B. 46.88
- C. 49.40

**Q5**: The interquartile range is closest to:
- A. 20.76
- B. 23.62
- C. 25.52
