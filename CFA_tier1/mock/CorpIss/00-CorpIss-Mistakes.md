## Corporate Issuers | M01 Organizational Forms - owner liability comparison
- error_type: concept_confusion
- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem
- source_type: screenshot
- wrong_choice_or_output: A. sole trader.
- correct_resolution: Correct answer is B. A sole trader retains all return but also assumes all business risk personally. A general partner in a limited partnership has unlimited liability and manages the business. Among these choices, a partner in a general partnership has the least exposure because business risk is shared across the partners, even though general partnership liability can still be significant.

## Corporate Issuers | M03 Corporate Governance - principal-agent manager-shareholder conflict
- error_type: concept_gap
- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem
- source_type: screenshot
- wrong_choice_or_output: Answered by discussing broader stakeholder views, ROE effects, debt financing, equity issuance and shareholder income dilution, but did not identify the principal-agent relationship or manager self-interest channels.
- correct_resolution: Managers are agents of shareholders but may put their own interests ahead of shareholders: insufficient effort, excessive perquisite consumption such as corporate jets or elaborate offices, failure to take appropriate risks, or investments/actions to safeguard their jobs. Independent board oversight and compensation alignment, including performance-based and equity-based compensation, help mitigate the conflict.

## Corporate Issuers | M02 Investors and Other Stakeholders - supplier relationship and leverage risk
- error_type: concept_gap
- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem
- source_type: screenshot
- wrong_choice_or_output: Answered that high leverage may destroy the company and make it unable to pay for goods delivered, but missed the supplier-specific stakeholder logic: short-term credit exposure plus long-term relationship-specific investment in integrated systems.
- correct_resolution: The supplier is a stakeholder because it has a vested interest in the company survival and success. It extends short-term credit through 30-day payment terms and has a long-term interest because it invested in integrated systems. High financial leverage is inconsistent with supplier interests because it raises short-term payment/default risk and longer-term risk to the value of the supplier relationship-specific investment.
