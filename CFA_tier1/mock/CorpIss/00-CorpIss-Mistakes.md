## Corporate Issuers | M04 Working Capital and Liquidity - liquidity drags and pulls
- error_type: concept_confusion
- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem
- source_type: screenshot
- wrong_choice_or_output: C. Early payments to vendors.
- correct_resolution: Correct answer is B. Uncollected receivables are a drag on liquidity because receipts lag and available funds decrease. Liquidity drags arise when expected cash receipts are delayed or uncertain, including uncollected receivables. Early payments to vendors are a pull on liquidity because disbursements are paid too quickly or trade credit is limited, requiring cash outflows before cash receipts arrive.

- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem

## Corporate Issuers | M05 Capital Investments and Capital Allocation - real options classification
- error_type: concept_confusion
- question_source: CFA Learning Ecosystem
- source_type: screenshot
- wrong_choice_or_output: C. flexibility option.
- correct_resolution: Correct answer is A. sizing option. Sizing options include abandonment or expansion of capacity after investment. If financial results are disappointing, abandoning the project is an abandonment option, which is part of sizing options. Timing options involve delaying or sequencing projects. Flexibility options involve operational changes using current capacity, such as overtime or adding shifts, not abandoning the investment.

## Corporate Issuers | M05 Capital Investments and Capital Allocation - ROIC criterion versus IRR and NPV
