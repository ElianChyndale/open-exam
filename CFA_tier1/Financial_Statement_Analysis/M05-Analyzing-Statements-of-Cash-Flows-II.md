# M05: Analyzing Statements of Cash Flows II

> **模块定位 Module role**: move from cash flow preparation to cash flow interpretation, free cash flow, and cash-based performance/coverage ratios.

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

**Official module**: Module 5: Analyzing Statements of Cash Flows II
**Exam task**: analyze reported/common-size cash flow statements and calculate/interpret FCFF, FCFE, performance ratios, and coverage cash flow ratios.

Learning outcome actions:

- Analyze and interpret reported and common-size cash flow statements.
- Calculate and interpret free cash flow to the firm, free cash flow to equity, and cash flow ratios.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M05 asks whether cash flows support earnings, reinvestment, debt service, and equity value. It is less about building the statement and more about answering: **Where did cash come from? Where did it go? Is operating cash flow sustainable?**

Core English anchors: sources and uses of cash, common-size cash flow statement, FCFF, FCFE, cash flow ratios, performance cash flow ratios, coverage cash flow ratios, cash interest coverage.

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Sources and uses**: evaluate major sources/uses and primary determinants of CFO, CFI, and CFF.
2. **Common-size cash flow**: scale cash flows by revenue or total cash inflows depending on prompt.
3. **Free cash flow**: FCFF for all capital providers; FCFE for common shareholders.
4. **Cash flow ratios**: performance and coverage ratios test quality and solvency.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

| Prompt Signal | Translate To | Answer Move |
|---|---|---|
| "cash flow available to all providers" | FCFF | Include after-tax interest; subtract fixed capital and working capital investment |
| "available to common shareholders" | FCFE | Start from CFO, subtract FCInv, add net borrowing |
| Large CFO but large CFI outflow | reinvestment stage | Evaluate whether capex is growth/maintenance and financed sustainably |
| CFO below NI | earnings quality concern | Investigate working capital, noncash revenue, capitalization, one-offs |
| Coverage ratio | debt service ability | Use CFO-based numerator, not accrual earnings unless stated |

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与判断台

| Formula / Framework | Use |
|---|---|
| FCFF = NI + NCC + Int(1 - tax rate) - FCInv - WCInv | Cash available to debt and equity providers |
| FCFF = CFO + Int(1 - tax rate) - FCInv | Shortcut from CFO |
| FCFE = CFO - FCInv + net borrowing | Cash available to common equity holders |
| CFO to revenue = CFO / revenue | Cash generation from sales |
| CFO to average assets = CFO / average total assets | Cash return on assets |
| Cash interest coverage = (CFO + interest paid + taxes paid) / interest paid | Ability to cover interest with cash |
| Cash debt coverage = CFO / total debt | Cash-based debt support |

Decision bench:

```text
FCFF asks before debt payments.
FCFE asks after debt financing effects.
Negative FCF is not automatically bad; interpret growth, lifecycle, and financing.
```

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

| Trap | Fix Rule |
|---|---|
| FCFF treated as equity cash flow | FCFF belongs to all capital providers; add back after-tax interest |
| Net borrowing omitted from FCFE | FCFE includes debt financing available to equity |
| Negative FCF automatically labeled weak | Check growth investment and financing context |
| CFO ratio interpreted alone | Compare with NI, revenue, assets, debt, and trend |
| Common-size basis assumed | Use the denominator stated in prompt; otherwise explain convention |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- FCFF = CFO + after-tax interest - FCInv.
- FCFE = CFO - FCInv + net borrowing.
- Cash flow analysis asks sustainability, not just arithmetic.
- CFO persistently below NI is a quality warning.
- Coverage ratios test cash ability to service obligations.
