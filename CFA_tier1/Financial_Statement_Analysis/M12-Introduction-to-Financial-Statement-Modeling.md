# M12: Introduction to Financial Statement Modeling

> **模块定位 Module role**: convert historical statements and strategic assumptions into a linked sales-based pro forma model.

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

**Official module**: Module 12: Introduction to Financial Statement Modeling
**Exam task**: develop a sales-based pro forma model, explain analyst forecast biases and remedies, connect competitive factors to prices/costs, model inflation/deflation, and choose forecast horizons/terminal projections.

Learning outcome actions:

- Demonstrate development of a sales-based pro forma company model.
- Explain behavioral factors affecting analyst forecasts and recommend remedies.
- Explain how Porter five forces and competitive position affect prices and costs.
- Forecast industry/company sales and costs under inflation or deflation.
- Explain explicit forecast horizon and long-term projection choices.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

M12 is where FSA becomes forward-looking. The exam expects a structured driver model: start with revenue, then operating costs, taxes, working capital, capex, depreciation, financing, and valuation inputs. Forecasts must also account for analyst bias and competitive economics.

Core English anchors: pro forma model, sales forecast, COGS, SG&A, operating profit by segment, tax forecast, shares outstanding, capex, depreciation, working capital, valuation inputs, overconfidence, conservatism bias, representativeness bias, confirmation bias, Porter five forces, inflation, forecast horizon.

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Build the model**: company overview, revenue forecast, COGS, SG&A, segment operating profit, non-operating items, taxes, shares, pro forma statements.
2. **Statement links**: cash flow, capex/depreciation, working capital, balance sheet, valuation inputs.
3. **Behavioral finance**: overconfidence, illusion of control, conservatism, representativeness, confirmation bias.
4. **Competitive factors**: industry structure and company position affect price and cost forecasts.
5. **Inflation/deflation**: distinguish industry volume, company market share, price changes, and cost changes.
6. **Forecast horizon**: explicit period and long-term normalized assumptions.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

| Prompt Signal | Translate To | Answer Move |
|---|---|---|
| "sales-based model" | revenue-first pro forma | Forecast sales, then margins, working capital, capex, depreciation |
| "overconfidence" | forecast bias | Use base rates, scenario analysis, feedback, and independent review |
| "conservatism bias" | slow update | Force evidence-based revision of prior assumptions |
| "Porter" | competitive pressure | Link rivalry/bargaining power/substitutes/new entrants to prices and costs |
| "inflation" | price and cost driver | Separate nominal growth into volume, price, and cost inflation |
| "forecast horizon" | explicit period choice | Use until firm reaches stable/normalized economics |

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与判断台

| Formula / Framework | Use |
|---|---|
| Revenue = units sold x average selling price | Base sales driver |
| Sales growth = volume growth + price growth + mix effects | Decompose revenue forecast |
| Gross profit = revenue - COGS | Margin forecast |
| Operating income = gross profit - SG&A - other operating expenses | Segment/operating forecast |
| Working capital investment | Forecast AR, inventory, AP as ratios/days of sales or COGS |
| Capex and depreciation forecast | Link long-term asset base to cash flow and balance sheet |
| Shares outstanding | EPS and valuation input |

Decision bench:

```text
Industry demand -> Company share/price -> Operating costs -> Taxes -> Working capital/capex -> Cash flow -> Balance sheet -> Valuation inputs
```

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

| Trap | Fix Rule |
|---|---|
| Model starts with net income | Start with sales and operating drivers |
| Revenue growth treated as one number | Decompose into volume, price, mix, market share, and inflation |
| Bias remedy too vague | Pair each bias with process control: base rates, scenarios, review, feedback |
| Competitive forces ignored | Prices and costs depend on rivalry, substitutes, entrants, supplier/customer power |
| Forecast horizon arbitrary | Explicit horizon should extend until normalized/stable economics are plausible |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Sales-based model starts with revenue drivers, not EPS.
- Forecast the operating engine first: revenue, COGS, SG&A, taxes, working capital, capex, depreciation.
- Biases: overconfidence, illusion of control, conservatism, representativeness, confirmation.
- Inflation/deflation requires separating volume and price/cost effects.
- Forecast horizon ends when long-term normalized assumptions are defensible.
