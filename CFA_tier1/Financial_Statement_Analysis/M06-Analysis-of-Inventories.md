# M06: Analysis of Inventories

> **模块定位 Module role**: translate inventory measurement and cost-flow choices into COGS, gross margin, assets, taxes, and turnover ratios.

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

**Official module**: Module 6: Analysis of Inventories
**Exam task**: describe inventory measurement at lower of cost and net realizable value, explain inflation/deflation effects under different valuation methods, and analyze inventory disclosures/ratios.

Learning outcome actions:

- Describe lower of cost and net realizable value and its statement/ratio implications.
- Calculate and explain inflation/deflation effects on COGS, inventory, gross margin, income, and ratios.
- Describe presentation/disclosure requirements and analyst issues in inventory analysis.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Inventory is a timing and comparability topic. The exam often changes price environment or cost-flow method, then asks what happens to COGS, ending inventory, profit, taxes, current ratio, inventory turnover, and days of inventory on hand.

Core English anchors: inventory valuation, lower of cost and net realizable value, FIFO, weighted average cost, LIFO, inflation, deflation, COGS, gross margin, inventory turnover, days of inventory on hand.

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Inventory valuation**: measurement at lower of cost and NRV; write-down implications.
2. **Inflation/deflation**: price trends drive differences between cost-flow methods.
3. **Presentation/disclosure**: policies, carrying amounts, write-downs, reversals, pledged inventory.
4. **Inventory ratios**: turnover and DOH diagnose efficiency and obsolescence risk.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

| Prompt Signal | Translate To | Answer Move |
|---|---|---|
| Rising prices | FIFO vs LIFO contrast | FIFO COGS lower, ending inventory higher, gross profit higher than LIFO |
| Falling prices | Reverse inflation pattern | FIFO COGS higher and ending inventory lower than LIFO |
| Write-down to NRV | loss recognition | Inventory/assets/equity/NI down; turnover may rise mechanically |
| Reversal under IFRS | inventory write-down reversal | Allowed up to original cost; US GAAP prohibits inventory reversal |
| DOH rising | inventory risk | Slower sales, obsolete inventory, demand weakness, or stock build |

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与判断台

| Formula / Framework | Use |
|---|---|
| Ending inventory = goods available for sale - COGS | Inventory roll-forward |
| Gross profit = revenue - COGS | Margin effect of method choice |
| Inventory turnover = COGS / average inventory | Activity ratio |
| DOH = 365 / inventory turnover | Days inventory held |
| Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities | Inventory method can affect liquidity |
| Lower of cost and NRV | Write down if NRV below cost |

Decision bench:

```text
Price trend -> Cost-flow method -> COGS/end inventory -> NI/tax/assets/equity -> ratios
```

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

| Trap | Fix Rule |
|---|---|
| Rising prices: FIFO COGS assumed higher | Under rising prices, FIFO uses older cheaper costs in COGS |
| Write-down treated as cash flow | Write-down is noncash; it affects NI/assets/equity |
| Turnover improvement after write-down overpraised | Lower inventory denominator can mechanically raise turnover |
| IFRS/US GAAP reversal confused | IFRS permits reversal of inventory write-down; US GAAP does not |
| Ignore tax effects | Higher COGS lowers taxable income and taxes if tax accounting follows |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Rising prices: FIFO -> lower COGS, higher inventory, higher profit than LIFO.
- Inventory turnover = COGS / average inventory; DOH = 365 / turnover.
- Write-down lowers assets, NI, equity; CFO impact depends on tax treatment and indirect add-back context.
- Disclosure review: method, write-downs, reversals, carrying value, pledged inventory.
