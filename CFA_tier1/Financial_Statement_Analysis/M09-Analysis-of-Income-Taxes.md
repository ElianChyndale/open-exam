# M09: Analysis of Income Taxes

> **模块定位 Module role**: reconcile accounting profit, taxable income, tax expense, taxes payable, DTA/DTL, and tax-rate disclosures.

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

**Official module**: Module 9: Analysis of Income Taxes
**Exam task**: contrast accounting profit/taxable income/taxes payable/tax expense, explain DTA/DTL creation and analysis, calculate tax rates, and analyze deferred tax/effective-rate disclosures.

Learning outcome actions:

- Contrast accounting profit, taxable income, taxes payable, income tax expense, temporary differences, and permanent differences.
- Explain DTA/DTL creation and analytical treatment.
- Calculate and interpret effective, statutory, and cash tax rates.
- Analyze deferred tax and effective tax rate reconciliation disclosures.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Income tax analysis is a reconciliation problem. The exam usually asks whether a difference is temporary or permanent, whether it creates a DTA or DTL, and how tax rates explain sustainability of earnings and cash flows.

Core English anchors: accounting profit, taxable income, taxes payable, income tax expense, temporary difference, permanent difference, taxable temporary difference, deductible temporary difference, deferred tax liability, deferred tax asset, valuation allowance, effective tax rate.

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Accounting profit vs taxable income**: financial reporting rules and tax rules differ.
2. **Temporary vs permanent differences**: temporary differences reverse; permanent differences do not.
3. **DTA/DTL**: deductible temporary differences create DTA; taxable temporary differences create DTL.
4. **Corporate tax rates**: statutory, effective, and cash tax rates answer different questions.
5. **Presentation/disclosure**: tax footnotes, rate reconciliation, DTA realizability, valuation allowance.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

| Prompt Signal | Translate To | Answer Move |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue recognized for accounting before tax | taxable temporary difference | Usually DTL |
| Expense deductible for tax before accounting | taxable temporary difference | Usually DTL |
| Expense recognized for accounting before tax deduction | deductible temporary difference | Usually DTA |
| Municipal tax-exempt income / nondeductible expense | permanent difference | Affects effective tax rate, no DTA/DTL |
| DTA realizability concern | valuation allowance / reduce asset | Assess future taxable income |

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与判断台

| Formula / Framework | Use |
|---|---|
| Income tax expense = taxes payable + change in DTL - change in DTA | Connect accrual tax expense to cash taxes |
| Effective tax rate = income tax expense / pretax income | Accounting tax burden |
| Cash tax rate = taxes paid / pretax income | Cash tax burden |
| Statutory tax rate | Legal benchmark for reconciliation |
| DTL | Future taxable amount from taxable temporary difference |
| DTA | Future deductible amount from deductible temporary difference |

Decision bench:

```text
Difference between accounting and tax -> Temporary or permanent -> Taxable or deductible -> DTL or DTA -> Rate/cash/ratio impact
```

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

| Trap | Fix Rule |
|---|---|
| All tax/book differences create DTA/DTL | Only temporary differences create deferred taxes |
| Taxes payable equals income tax expense | Taxes payable is current tax liability; tax expense is accrual accounting |
| DTA always high quality | DTA depends on future taxable income and realizability |
| Effective tax rate used as cash tax rate | Use income tax expense for ETR and taxes paid for cash tax rate |
| DTL automatically treated like debt | Analyze expected reversal and economic substance |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Temporary differences reverse; permanent differences do not.
- Taxable temporary difference -> DTL; deductible temporary difference -> DTA.
- Tax expense = taxes payable plus deferred tax effects.
- ETR = tax expense / pretax income; cash tax rate = taxes paid / pretax income.
- DTA quality depends on realizability.
