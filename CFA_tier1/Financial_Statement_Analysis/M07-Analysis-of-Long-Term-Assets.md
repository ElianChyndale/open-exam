# M07: Analysis of Long-Term Assets

> **模块定位 Module role**: evaluate how long-term asset recognition, depreciation/amortization, impairment, derecognition, and disclosures affect profitability and asset efficiency.

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

**Official module**: Module 7: Analysis of Long-Term Assets
**Exam task**: compare financial reporting for intangible assets by source, evaluate impairment/derecognition effects for PP&E and intangibles, and analyze disclosures.

Learning outcome actions:

- Compare purchased, internally developed, and business-combination intangible assets.
- Explain and evaluate impairment/derecognition effects on statements and ratios.
- Analyze PP&E and intangible asset disclosures.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Long-term asset questions test the difference between **capitalized cost, subsequent expense, carrying value, impairment, and disposal gains/losses**. The analyst task is to separate operating economics from accounting timing.

Core English anchors: PP&E, intangible assets, purchased intangibles, internally developed intangibles, business combination, depreciation, amortization, impairment, reversal, derecognition, carrying amount.

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. **Intangible acquisition source**: purchased outside business combination, internally developed, or acquired in business combination.
2. **Impairment**: PP&E, finite-life intangibles, indefinite-life intangibles, assets held for sale, and reversals.
3. **Derecognition**: sale, retirement, disposal other than sale.
4. **Presentation/disclosure**: useful lives, methods, gross carrying amount, accumulated depreciation/amortization, impairment, commitments.
5. **Disclosure analysis**: infer asset age, reinvestment, useful-life estimates, and comparability.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试翻译

| Prompt Signal | Translate To | Answer Move |
|---|---|---|
| Purchased intangible | capitalized asset | Amortize if finite-lived; test impairment |
| Internally developed brand/reputation | usually expensed | Do not capitalize unless specific criteria are met |
| Impairment loss | carrying value write-down | Assets/NI/equity down; ROA may improve later from lower asset base |
| Reversal | standard-specific | IFRS may allow certain reversals; goodwill reversal prohibited |
| Sale of asset | derecognition | Gain/loss = proceeds - carrying amount; proceeds in CFI |

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与判断台

| Formula / Framework | Use |
|---|---|
| Carrying amount = historical cost - accumulated depreciation/amortization - impairments | Book value before disposal/ratio analysis |
| Gain/loss on sale = proceeds - carrying amount | Derecognition effect |
| Average age = accumulated depreciation / depreciation expense | Asset age estimate |
| Average useful life = gross PP&E / depreciation expense | Useful-life estimate |
| Remaining useful life = net PP&E / depreciation expense | Reinvestment clue |
| Fixed asset turnover = revenue / average net fixed assets | Asset efficiency |

Decision bench:

```text
Acquisition source -> Capitalize or expense -> Subsequent depreciation/amortization -> Impairment/derecognition -> Ratio impact
```

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

| Trap | Fix Rule |
|---|---|
| Disposal gain uses historical cost | Use carrying amount after accumulated depreciation/amortization and impairment |
| Impairment viewed only as income effect | Also reduce assets/equity and alter future depreciation/ROA |
| Goodwill reversal allowed | Goodwill impairment reversal is prohibited |
| Asset age ratios overinterpreted | Estimates depend on depreciation method, asset mix, and additions/disposals |
| Internally generated intangible capitalized broadly | Apply strict criteria; many internally generated intangibles are expensed |

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Carrying amount is the anchor for impairment and disposal.
- Purchased intangibles can be recognized; internally generated intangibles are constrained.
- Impairment lowers assets, NI, equity; future depreciation may be lower.
- Disposal gain/loss = proceeds - carrying amount.
- Disclosure analysis helps infer asset age, useful life, and reinvestment.
