# M03: Market Efficiency

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: market efficiency, market vs intrinsic value, efficiency drivers, weak/semi-strong/strong forms, EMH implications, anomalies, behavioral finance.
- **C+ output**: 能把信息集和交易成本转成 active/passive, technical/fundamental strategy implications。
- **Evidence anchor**: V5 Module 3, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Market efficiency 不是说价格永远正确，而是说在给定信息集和成本下，abnormal profit 很难持续。考试核心是信息集边界：past prices, public information, or all information。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Define efficient market and separate market value from intrinsic value.
2. Identify drivers: participants, information availability, disclosure, trading limits, transaction and information costs.
3. Compare weak, semi-strong and strong forms.
4. Translate each form into technical analysis, fundamental analysis and portfolio management implications.
5. Classify anomalies: time-series, cross-sectional, closed-end fund discounts, earnings surprise, IPOs.
6. Use behavioral finance to explain why anomalies may appear or persist.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `Past price pattern earns abnormal returns` -> violates weak-form efficiency.
- `Public announcement can still be used for abnormal returns` -> violates semi-strong efficiency.
- `Insider/private information cannot earn abnormal returns` -> strong-form claim.
- `High transaction costs, short-sale constraints, poor disclosure` -> lower efficiency.
- `Herding/overconfidence/loss aversion` -> behavioral explanation, not a calculation.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Market vs intrinsic value | market value is observed price; intrinsic value is estimated by analysis | Mispricing claim requires an estimate. |
| Weak form | prices reflect past market data | Technical analysis should not consistently work. |
| Semi-strong form | prices reflect all public information | Fundamental public-info analysis should not consistently work. |
| Strong form | prices reflect public and private information | Even insiders cannot earn abnormal returns. |
| Active/passive implication | More efficient market -> stronger case for passive, lower fees | Inefficiency alone does not guarantee exploitable profit after costs. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Expected item types: classify efficiency form, identify anomaly, interpret cost/friction effect, choose investment strategy implication.
- Review tag: `Equity-M03-efficiency`.
- Miss log fields: information set, analysis method, anomaly type, friction, behavioral bias.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Semi-strong includes weak-form information because past prices are public。
- An anomaly is not automatically profitable after transaction and information costs。
- Intrinsic value is an estimate; market value is observable。
- Behavioral finance explains deviations but does not repeal all market discipline。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Weak = past prices.
- Semi-strong = all public info.
- Strong = all public and private info.
- Efficiency depends on information + costs + limits to trading.
