# M06: Industry and Competitive Analysis

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: purposes and steps of industry analysis, classification methods, industry survey, Porter Five Forces, PESTLE, competitive strategy and position.
- **C+ output**: 能把 industry structure 转成 profitability, growth and risk implications。
- **Evidence anchor**: V5 Module 6, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Industry analysis explains why similar companies may have different sustainable margins and growth. 本模块是 M07 forecast 和 M08 valuation 的 external driver layer。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Purposes: improve forecasts, identify opportunities, understand risks.
2. Classification: third-party schemes and alternative grouping methods.
3. Industry survey: size, historical growth, life-cycle, profitability, market share, major players.
4. Industry structure: Porter Five Forces.
5. External influences: PESTLE.
6. Competitive positioning: cost leadership, differentiation, focus, strategy durability.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `low barriers to entry` -> threat of new entrants high -> pressure on profitability.
- `many substitutes` -> pricing power lower.
- `few powerful buyers/suppliers` -> margin pressure.
- `fragmented competitors, slow growth` -> rivalry high.
- `PESTLE` -> external macro/regulatory/social/tech/legal/environmental forces, not direct competitor force.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Framework | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Industry classification | products/services, business-cycle sensitivity, supply chain, statistical grouping, third-party schemes | Classification affects peer selection. |
| Industry survey | size, growth, margins, market share trends, concentration | Use data to support forecast assumptions. |
| Porter Five Forces | entrants, substitutes, buyers, suppliers, rivalry | Stronger force generally lowers industry profitability. |
| PESTLE | political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental | External influences may affect demand, costs or regulation. |
| Competitive position | cost leadership, differentiation, focus | Position can offset unattractive industry structure. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Expected item types: classify industry method, identify strongest force, apply PESTLE, infer profitability effect, compare competitive position.
- Review tag: `Equity-M06-industry-analysis`.
- Miss log fields: classification method, force, external influence, profitability direction, company position.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Porter Five Forces analyze industry structure; PESTLE analyzes external environment。
- High growth does not automatically mean high profitability if rivalry or entry threats are high。
- Supplier power and buyer power are opposite sides of bargaining pressure; identify who has leverage。
- Classification schemes help comparison but can misclassify diversified firms。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Five Forces answer: who pressures margins?
- PESTLE answer: what outside force changes demand/cost/risk?
- Industry survey answer: how big, how fast, how profitable, who wins?
- Competitive position answer: why this firm earns more or less than peers.
