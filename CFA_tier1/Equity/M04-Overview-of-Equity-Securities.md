# M04: Overview of Equity Securities

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: equity security characteristics, voting/ownership rights, common vs preference shares, public/private equity, non-domestic investing, risk-return, market vs book value, cost of equity vs ROE.
- **C+ output**: 能判断一类 equity claim 的 cash-flow priority, control rights, liquidity and risk-return implication。
- **Evidence anchor**: V5 Module 4, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Equity securities are residual ownership claims, but not all equity has the same voting power, dividend priority or liquidity. 本模块是 M08 valuation 的 claim-definition layer。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Importance of equity securities in financing and investing.
2. Common shares: residual claim, voting rights, variable dividends.
3. Preference shares: dividend priority and hybrid debt/equity characteristics.
4. Private vs public equity: liquidity, disclosure, valuation and ownership concentration.
5. Non-domestic access: direct investing, ADR, GDR, global registered shares, baskets.
6. Risk and return: equity risk, return upside, priority in liquidation.
7. Value concepts: market value, book value, cost of equity, ROE, required return.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `cumulative preference` -> unpaid dividends accumulate before common dividends.
- `participating preference` -> may receive extra dividends beyond stated amount.
- `convertible preference` -> option to convert to common shares.
- `public equity` -> better liquidity and price transparency than private equity.
- `ADR/GDR` -> indirect non-domestic investing through depository receipts.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Decision rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Common vs preference | common has residual control/upside; preference has dividend priority | Preference often has limited voting rights. |
| Public vs private | public = exchange listing, liquidity, disclosure; private = negotiated, illiquid, concentrated | Do not assume private is always lower risk; liquidity and transparency differ. |
| Market value vs book value | market value reflects investors' current pricing; book value reflects accounting equity | Book value is not intrinsic value. |
| Cost of equity vs ROE | cost of equity is required return; ROE is accounting return | Compare to judge value creation, not to define price. |
| Non-domestic route | direct, depository receipt, global registered share, basket | Consider FX/country/liquidity risks. |

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Preference shares are equity securities, not bonds, even when dividends are fixed。
- Voting rights and cash-flow priority are separate dimensions。
- Public equity has more price transparency; private equity valuation is often negotiated and less observable。
- ROE is not investors' required return。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Common = residual claim + voting + upside.
- Preference = dividend priority + limited control.
- Public = liquid/transparent; private = negotiated/illiquid.
- Cost of equity = market-required return; ROE = accounting performance.
