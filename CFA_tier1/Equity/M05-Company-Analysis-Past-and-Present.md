# M05: Company Analysis: Past and Present

## 0. Reading Contract 阅读契约

- **Official scope**: research report elements, business model, revenue and pricing power, operating profitability, working capital, capital investments and capital structure.
- **C+ output**: 能把 historical company facts 转成 forecast drivers and valuation inputs。
- **Evidence anchor**: V5 Module 5, practice and solutions available in the 2026 ePub index.

## 1. Module Brief 模块定位

Company analysis connects facts to forecasts. 本模块不要求完整估值模型，而要求识别 revenue, margins, working capital, capex and financing 的 key drivers。

## 2. Curriculum Spine 教材主线

1. Research report: business description, industry, governance, financial analysis, valuation, risks.
2. Business model: how the company creates, delivers and captures value.
3. Revenue analysis: price, volume, mix, market share, growth, pricing power.
4. Operating profitability: cost behavior, cost classification, margins.
5. Working capital: receivables, inventory, payables and operating liquidity.
6. Capital investments and capital structure: sources and uses of capital, reinvestment and financing choices.

## 3. Exam Translation 考试转译

- `pricing power` -> ability to raise prices without losing significant volume/share.
- `fixed cost base` -> operating leverage; revenue changes magnify operating profit.
- `working capital investment` -> growth may consume cash even when accounting profit rises.
- `capital investment` -> supports capacity, growth or efficiency; evaluate with expected returns.
- `capital structure` -> affects risk, flexibility and required return.

## 4. Formula & Decision Bench 公式与决策台

| Trigger | Framework | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue drivers | `revenue = price x volume`; plus mix, market share, market growth, FX | Separate recurring from non-recurring drivers. |
| Pricing power | differentiation, brand, switching costs, regulation, supply constraints | High pricing power supports margins. |
| Cost behavior | fixed vs variable; natural vs functional classification | Fixed costs increase operating leverage. |
| Working capital | receivables + inventory - payables | More working capital often reduces near-term free cash flow. |
| Capital structure | debt/equity mix and maturity profile | Link to risk and cost of capital. |

## 5. Practice & Mock Evidence 题库证据

- Expected item types: identify research report component, infer revenue driver, classify cost behavior, interpret working capital and capital structure.
- Review tag: `Equity-M05-company-analysis`.
- Miss log fields: business model feature, revenue driver, cost driver, working capital effect, financing implication.

## 6. Trap Ledger 易错账本

- Revenue growth can come from price, volume, mix, acquisitions or FX; do not assume demand growth。
- Higher working capital may signal growth or inefficiency; use context。
- Operating profitability and cash generation are related but not identical。
- A good business model still needs industry and capital structure context。

## 7. Final Recall Sheet 终局速记

- Report spine: business -> industry -> financials -> valuation -> risks.
- Revenue = price x volume, adjusted for mix/share/market.
- Working capital growth consumes cash.
- Historical analysis is useful only when it sharpens forecast assumptions.
